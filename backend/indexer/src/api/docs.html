JACKTRADE API

JackTrade backend API

Everything the app reads and sends: live prices, trading through the relayer, and the indexed history of the protocol on Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663). Collateral is USDG; the reward token is JACKTRADE.

Three services, all public and CORS-open. Reads need no key. Trades and wallet actions are signed in the browser and sent to the relayer, which pays the gas.

Pick the service by what you need. Every service answers JSON and errors carry a stable code plus a human message in English.

How a trade happens

The wallet signs once to create a session key. After that, opening and closing are signed by the session key in the browser, with no popups and no gas for the user.

1 · relayerLoad configGET /config: contracts, EIP-712 types, market limits and chain time (use it for signedAt).
2 · walletDepositPOST /action kind depositPermit: a USDG permit signature, no approve, no gas.
3 · walletRegister a session keyPOST /action kind registerSessionKey, up to 30 days. Keep the private key in the browser only.
4 · session keyOpenPOST /intent kind open with a priceLimit, then poll GET /intent/:id until executed.
5 · indexerShow it/api/positions/:user (live PnL, liquidation price) and /api/preview/:id before closing.
6 · session keyClosePOST /intent kind close. Profit is paid now or queued if the house is short (see queue).
